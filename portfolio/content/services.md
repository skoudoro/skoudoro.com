I help companies and labs build, speed up and maintain the scientific software their R&D depends on. Trusted by industrial R&D teams. Invoices and contracts available.

## Who I work with

- Pharma & biotech imaging teams
- Medical-imaging and neuroimaging companies
- Simulation, CAE and 3D software vendors
- Research labs and imaging core facilities

## What I do

### Custom imaging & data pipelines

- Diffusion MRI, microscopy/FLIM and 3D mesh processing pipelines
- Reproducible, tested and documented, so your team can own them
- Integration with your existing tools, storage and compute

### Performance optimization

- Profiling to find the real bottlenecks
- Rewrites in Cython, SIMD or GPU where it matters, typically 10–100× faster
- Benchmarks before and after, on your own data

### File formats & data conversion

- NIfTI, DICOM, GIFTI, tractography and 3D mesh formats
- Readers and writers for proprietary or vendor formats
- Mesh I/O with [polyxios](https://polyxios.org), validated on your files

### Maintenance & support contracts

- Priority fixes and guaranteed response times
- Code review and architecture advice for internal tools
- Long-term support for code built on DIPY, FURY, polyxios and the scientific Python stack

## How we can work together

- **Audit**: a fixed-price review of your pipeline or codebase, with a written report of performance and correctness issues and a prioritized plan.
- **Sprint**: one or two weeks on a well-defined goal, such as a speedup, a new format or a pipeline, at a fixed scope and price.
- **Retainer**: a monthly block of hours for ongoing development, support and code review.
